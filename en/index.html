Questions

Short answers

How much does a landing page cost?

A landing page with design is 10 500–18 600 ₴. That is 35–62 hours at 300 ₴/hour, about 17 working days. Layout from your design starts at 6 300 ₴ (6 300–11 100 ₴, 21–37 hours), without design or copywriting. We lock the exact sum after the brief.

Is design included?

A landing page with design includes the brief, wireframe, copy within the brief, desktop and mobile UI, layout, a form, basic SEO, QA, launch and 2–4 hours of revisions. Layout from your design does not include design or copy: we start from a finished mockup. Hosting, a domain, paid stock photos, ads, a logo and content beyond the brief are not included.

How does payment work?

After the estimate, 50% upfront and the rest after acceptance. The rate is 300 ₴ per hour. Work outside the agreed hours is billed at this rate.

What about the warranty?

After delivery there are 14 days for small fixes inside the agreed scope. Support after these 14 days is agreed separately.

How long does a landing page take?

A landing page with design takes about 17 working days (35–62 hours). Layout from a finished design takes 21–37 hours. We name the timeline for other work after the brief.

Why a starting price, not a fixed one?

The hours on the tech card are a range, because the scope depends on the brief. Before we start we show the bounds. We name the exact price after the brief, before the prepayment.
