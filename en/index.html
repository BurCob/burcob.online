What the price does not include

  • Complex design and branding from scratch, a logo, full copywriting. You provide the design and the copy — or we estimate that separately.
  • Domain, hosting, VPS, SSL and paid plans for CRM, payment providers, n8n/Make.
  • Ads, SMM, filling the site with a large amount of content.
  • Mobile apps and integrations with 1C / BAS without an open API and documentation.
  • Support after the warranty window: 14 days of small fixes after delivery.
